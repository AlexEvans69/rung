# Privacy policy: Rung SERP Counter

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Rung is a browser extension that numbers organic results on Google Search pages and lets you attach notes to a page or website.

## What is stored

- the notes you write
- the badge colour you choose and the Numbering and Notes switches
- how many organic results each results page you visited had (so later pages can be numbered)

All of it is stored on your device, in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

## What is not collected

Rung does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It makes no network requests, has no accounts, no analytics and no third-party code. The developer has no access to your notes.

## Export and import

If you export your notes, the file is created on your device and goes only where you put it. Importing reads a file you choose, on your device.

## Permissions

Rung requests only the `storage` permission and runs only on Google Search results pages.

## Incognito

When you allow the extension in incognito windows, it behaves the same way and stores data the same way.

## Deleting your data

Delete a note in the note editor, or remove the extension to delete everything it stored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published in this repository with a new date.
